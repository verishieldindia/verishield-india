📖 Deep-Dive: Why Moonlighting Detection is Crucial for Remote Tech Teams

In the post-pandemic digital economy, remote work has unlocked unprecedented global talent pools, but it has simultaneously introduced severe vulnerabilities—chief among them being dual employment, commonly known as "moonlighting." When software engineers or financial analysts simultaneously commit to two full-time corporate contracts, enterprises face catastrophic risks regarding intellectual property theft, data leakage, code contamination, and severe productivity drop-offs. Traditional HR background checks only review past employment at the time of onboarding, leaving organizations completely blind to parallel employment during the tenure.

VeriShield India solves this critical enterprise risk by deploying automated EPFO (Employee Provident Fund Organisation) unified passbook history audits and multi-repository employment tracking. By continuously cross-referencing statutory social security contributions and UAN updates, our platform instantly flags simultaneous employer contributions, enabling compliance officers and HR leaders to neutralize hidden liabilities before data breaches occur. Safeguarding your proprietary assets requires more than a handshake; it demands institutionalized, real-time trust-tech infrastructure.

Background Verification (BGV) Glossary & Knowledge Base

A comprehensive guide for HR professionals and compliance officers explaining key technical terms used in modern candidate screening.

📖 UAN / EPFO Moonlighting Audit

The process of auditing a candidate's Universal Account Number (UAN) passbook history to detect parallel statutory contributions, identifying if an individual is simultaneously employed with multiple corporations.

📖 Pan-India e-Courts Litigation Check

A comprehensive judicial database scan covering district courts, high courts, and the Supreme Court of India to unearth any active civil or criminal legal proceedings involving the candidate.

📖 GPS Residential Geo-Tagging

A tamper-proof digital address verification method that captures precise latitude/longitude coordinates and a live rear-camera photograph of the candidate's residence.
