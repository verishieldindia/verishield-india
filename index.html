Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find comprehensive answers regarding enterprise background verification, candidate onboarding, compliance standards, and automated API security.

Q1. How does HR initiate candidate verification on the platform?

HR professionals log into the Enterprise HR Portal, input candidate details, select the appropriate background verification package, and generate a secure, locked verification link instantly.

Q2. Can candidates modify their screening package once generated?

No, the screening package is securely locked by HR during link generation. Candidates can only submit their personal information, identity credentials, and compliance documents.

Q3. Is PAN Card verification mandatory for all candidates?

Yes, PAN Card details are strictly mandatory to ensure tax compliance, financial verification, and primary identity cross-referencing across government databases.

Q4. How does real-time GPS Geo-Tagging work during onboarding?

The candidate's smartphone browser securely captures precise GPS coordinates (Latitude and Longitude) along with a live rear-camera photo of their residential entrance to prevent fraud.

Q5. Where are the final background verification reports delivered?

Comprehensive PDF reports containing digital signatures, secure QR verification codes, and audit trails are sent directly to the HR's registered corporate email ID.

Q6. What advanced checks are included in the All-in-One Corporate Package?

It includes PAN KYC, Pan-India e-Courts criminal/civil litigation checks, EPFO/UAN unified passbook history for moonlighting detection, university degree authentication, and GPS geo-tagging.

Q7. Is candidate legal consent required before screening?

Yes, every candidate must explicitly check the mandatory legal consent box confirming that all submitted details are accurate and authorizing compliance screening.

Q8. How long does the complete verification process take?

Automated database checks and ID KYC are executed instantly upon submission, while comprehensive multi-tier reports are delivered within 24 to 48 business hours.

Q9. Does VeriShield India comply with data privacy regulations?

Yes, we strictly adhere to global data privacy frameworks, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, and stringent corporate confidentiality protocols.

Q10. How do we handle candidate data privacy after report delivery?

We enforce a strict Zero-Data Retention Policy. All raw candidate personal files and documents are automatically and permanently erased from active servers once reports are sent to HR.
