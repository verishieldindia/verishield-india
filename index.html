Privacy Policy & Zero Data Retention

Data Protection & Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Mandate

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information strictly required to execute background verification: corporate HR name and email, candidate name, parents' names, phone number, email address, residential address, PAN number, secondary government ID details, live selfie image, live GPS coordinates, and explicit consent.

2. Purpose Limitation

Collected data is used solely for conducting authorized background verification checks and delivering the encrypted audit report to the requesting corporate HR. VeriShield India never sells, rents, or uses candidate personal data for advertising or unrelated third-party marketing.

3. Explicit Candidate Consent

Verification submissions are processed only after the candidate actively checks the mandatory legal consent declaration and grants device permissions for live camera and GPS location capture.

4. Zero Data Retention (ZDR) & Data Erasure

Under our Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Policy, once the final verification report is dispatched to the authorized HR email ID, sensitive raw candidate identifiers (PAN, secondary ID number, residential address, and GPS coordinates) are permanently purged/redacted from active storage.

5. Security & Data Subject Requests

All platform communications are encrypted over HTTPS. Candidates or corporate clients seeking data access, correction, or immediate erasure may contact verishieldindia@gmail.com with their Tracking ID.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What is Background Verification (BGV) and why is it needed?

BGV is the structured process of verifying a candidate's identity, address/GPS location, litigation history, and employment credentials to ensure safe, compliant hiring.

Q2. Does VeriShield India have a physical walk-in office?

No. VeriShield India operates as a 100% Virtual Office with Pan-India digital operations. All HR requests, candidate verifications, and support are handled online.

Q3. How does HR initiate a candidate verification?

HR submits the candidate's basic details via the HR Portal (Single or Bulk CSV). The system generates a unique Tracking ID and a secure candidate verification link.

Q4. What documents and steps are mandatory for the candidate?

Candidates must provide a mandatory PAN Card number, a secondary government ID (Aadhaar, DigiLocker, Voter ID, or Driving License), a live selfie, live GPS coordinates, and explicit consent.

Q5. Why are Live Selfie and GPS Geo-Tagging required?

Capturing real-time Lat/Lng coordinates along with a live portrait prevents proxy submissions and verifies physical location authenticity.

Q6. How does Zero Data Retention (ZDR) protect candidate privacy?

Once the final report is shared with HR, raw sensitive identifiers (PAN, secondary ID number, residential address, and GPS data) are permanently redacted/wiped from active storage.

Q7. Does this platform work on both Android and Apple devices?

Yes. The interface is built with mobile-first responsive design, safe-area support, and native camera/GPS prompts for both Android and iPhone/iPad browsers.

Q8. How can I contact VeriShield India for quotes or support?

Email us at verishieldindia@gmail.com or click the floating Live Help chat button at the bottom right of the screen.
