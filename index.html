Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How does HR initiate a candidate verification?

The HR logs into the Enterprise HR Portal, enters candidate details, selects the screening package, and generates a secure, locked link.

Q2. Can the candidate change the verification package?

No, the package is securely locked by the HR during link generation. The candidate can only submit personal details and verification documents.

Q3. Is PAN Card mandatory for all candidates?

Yes, PAN Card is strictly mandatory for tax compliance and primary identity cross-referencing.

Q4. How does real-time Geo-Tagging work?

The candidate's smartphone browser securely captures precise GPS coordinates (Latitude & Longitude) along with a live rear-camera photo of their residential entrance.

Q5. Where does the final verification report get delivered?

The comprehensive PDF report containing digital signatures and secure QR codes is sent directly to the HR's registered email ID.

Q6. What checks are included in the All-in-One Corporate Package?

It includes PAN KYC, e-Courts criminal litigation scan, EPFO/UAN employment history & moonlighting check, university education degree verification, and geo-tagging.

Q7. Is candidate consent legally required?

Yes, every candidate must explicitly check the legal consent box before submitting their onboarding and verification data.

Q8. How long does the screening process take?

Automated database and ID checks are processed instantly upon HR payment confirmation, while comprehensive reports are delivered within 24-48 hours.

Q9. Does VeriShield India comply with data privacy norms?

Yes, we strictly adhere to global data privacy standards, the DPDP Act, and secure handling of candidate Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Q10. How can HR track ongoing cases?

HR receives automated status updates and can access enterprise screening records directly through their dedicated portal access.
