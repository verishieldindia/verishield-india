Frequently Asked Questions (FDR / FAQ)

Q1. What is background verification (BGV)?

It is the systematic process of verifying educational, professional, and personal credentials.

Q2. Is candidate data secure?

Yes, under our strict Zero Data Retention (ZDR) policy, all raw files are permanently erased.

Q3. How does HR initiate candidate screening?

HR fills out the single or bulk onboarding form via the corporate portal.

Q4. Can candidates edit their pre-filled details?

Candidates can update contact info, parents' names, and addresses while core identifiers remain locked.

Q5. Which identity documents are mandatory?

PAN card verification is strictly mandatory alongside secondary proofs like Aadhaar.

Q6. Why is GPS Geo-Tagging required?

It captures precise coordinates along with a live residential photo to prevent address fraud.

Q7. Who receives the final audit report?

Password-protected PDF reports are routed exclusively to corporate HR email IDs.

Q8. What does moonlighting detection entail?

It audits EPFO/UAN passbook history to detect parallel contributions.

Q9. What is included in e-Courts litigation screening?

It scans pan-India civil and criminal court registries.

Q10. How does the live case tracker function?

Users input their unique case tracking ID in the portal to review milestones.

Q11. Is bulk CSV candidate upload supported?

Yes, HR teams can process multiple candidates simultaneously via CSV uploads.

Q12. Who can I contact for technical assistance?

You can reach our enterprise support team via support@verishieldindia.com.

Q13. Are startups eligible to use this platform?

Yes, our verification modules scale flexibly for startups and enterprises.

Q14. How long does a standard verification take?

Automated checks and geo-tagging typically complete within 24 to 48 hours.

Q15. Is candidate consent legally required?

Yes, checking the explicit legal consent box is mandatory.

Q16. Can selfies be captured from mobile browsers?

Yes, the portal supports direct camera capture for live portraits.

Q17. How can agencies join the partner network?

Agencies can submit credentials directly to our corporate email.

Q18. Are audit reports encrypted?

Yes, final PDF reports delivered to HR are heavily encrypted.

Q19. Does VeriShield comply with data privacy acts?

Yes, we comply with global data privacy frameworks.

Q20. How does the live chat helpdesk work?

You can click the floating chat widget at the bottom right anytime.
